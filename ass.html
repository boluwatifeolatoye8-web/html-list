    Ingredients
  • 1 packet of spaghetti
  • Pepper
  • Tomato paste
  • onion (chopped)
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 curry powder
  • 1 Dried thyme
  • 2 seasoning cubes
  • Chicken or meat
  • Water and salt to taste

Step-by-Step Instructions on how to prepare it

Step 1
Blend the Pepper Mix: Blend the tomatoes, red bell pepper, scotch bonnet peppers, and half of the onion until smooth.Parboil the Spaghetti: Boil a large pot of water with a pinch of salt. Add the spaghetti and parboil for about 4 to 5 minutes so it is firm . Strain it through a sieve and set aside
Step 2
Fry the Tomato Paste: Heat the vegetable oil in a large pot or pan over medium heat. Add the remaining chopped onions and fry until translucent. Add the tomato paste and fry for about 5 minutes, stirring constantly to remove the sour taste and bring out a rich red color.Build the Jollof Sauce: Pour in the blended tomato and pepper mix. Add the curry powder, dried thyme, and seasoning cubes. Stir well and let the mixture fry for 10 to 15 minutes until the water dries up and the oil starts to float to the top
Step 3
Add Stock: Pour in your chicken or meat stock (and a little splash of reserved pasta water if needed). Stir and let the sauce simmer for 3 minutes. Taste and adjust the salt or seasoning.Combine and Simmer: Add the parboiled spaghetti directly into the simmering sauce. Toss thoroughly until every strand of pasta is well-coated with the red stew.
Step 4
Cover the pot tightly with a lid, turn the heat down to low, and let it steam for 5 minutes so the spaghetti absorbs the flavors completely.Serve: Fluff the Jollof spaghetti with a fork or tongs, and serve hot on its own or with your choice of fried chicken, fish, or beef.
